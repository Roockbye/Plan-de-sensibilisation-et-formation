## What is this about?

Criminals send fake messages.
These messages look like real messages.

They come:

- by email,
- by text message,
- with a QR code.

A QR code is a small black and white square.
You take a picture of it with your phone.
It opens a website.

The criminals want to:

- steal your password,
- steal money,
- damage computers.

## How to spot a fake message?

Be careful if the message:

- asks you to act **very fast**,
- makes you **afraid**,
- asks for your **password**,
- asks you to **pay**,
- promises you a **gift**.

Be careful: a fake message can be very well written.
It may have no mistakes.
